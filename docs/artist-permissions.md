## Permission request draft

Suggested subject — Request to use original Hatsune Miku artwork in Thong Khong Toolbox

Hello,

My name is Thong Khong. I develop Thong Khong Toolbox, a Windows desktop app currently available as a free download. It includes a tool for comparing work shifts with a user's calendar.

I would like your written confirmation that I may include the following original Hatsune Miku illustration as part of an optional Miku pink theme.

https://piapro.net/intl/images/popup_miku.jpg

The proposed use includes bundling the image for offline display in the Windows installer and portable EXE, distributing those files through GitHub Releases and my website, and showing screenshots of the theme on the product page. The illustration would be displayed at different sizes while preserving its proportions.

I will credit KEI and Crypton Future Media, Inc., and link to the original source and the applicable license in the app and repository. Please let me know your preferred credit wording and any additional conditions or fees.

App website
https://thongkhong.me/tool-box/

Source and downloads
https://github.com/thongteevee/thong-khong-toolbox

Please confirm whether your permission covers this use or direct me to the appropriate rights holder.

Thank you,
Thong Khong

## Record replies

For each reply, record the sender, date, exact artwork URL, permitted distribution and modifications, credit wording, any expiration, and whether permission covers commercial use. Retain the original reply privately. Public credits should contain the approved attribution and source links.
